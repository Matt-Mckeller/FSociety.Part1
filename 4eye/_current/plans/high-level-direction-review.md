## Decision Points / Branches

- **At week 3:** Did at least one Services contract land?
  - **Yes →** keep one contract running for cashflow, focus rest on 4eye flagship.
  - **No →** decide between (a) more Services outreach, (b) ask for support (dad/donations), (c) part-time job to stabilize.
- **At week 6:** Is Controller v1 actually pulling its weight (used in your daily work)?
  - **Yes →** worth starting V2 scoping.
  - **No →** freeze Controller, redirect time to App + Website polish.
- **At week 10:** Is 4eye Website + App presentation video ready to show?
  - **Yes →** start funding/cofounder outreach.
  - **No →** finish the artifacts before pitching — pitching half-baked burns relationships.
