# Scene 1 — Classroom: Engagement Transformation

> ⭐ **Template scene.** This file demonstrates the full structure used for every scene file.

**Duration target:**
- 75s master: ~22s (0:00–0:22)
- 30s cut: ~8s (compressed)
- 15s cut: hook + hero moment only
- 6s hook: hero moment + logo

**Scene promise:** Disengaged students become engaged through the introduction of 4eye / HUD. Empowerment cascades teacher → students.

**Primary concepts (from matrix):** C01 Engagement, C03 HUD/Product, C04 4wing, C05 Empowerment hand-off
**Secondary concepts:** C02 Growth, C10 Currency/reward, C18 Leaderboard, C20 Hate→understanding
**Tertiary / background:** C16 Old paper, C19 Tent comfort, C21 Gear, C23 Time
**Brand themes carried:** Improve, Innovate, Heal, Win

**Setting:** Modern classroom. Slightly futuristic but recognizable. Soft daylight. Slightly desaturated until HUD activates, then color floods in.

**Style anchors:** see [`../00-style-bible.md`](../00-style-bible.md) → Sections 2, 3, 4, 7.3

## Beat-by-Beat

### Beat 1.1 — Classroom Cold Open (0:00–0:03)
**Action:** Wide establishing shot. 6–8 students sprawled across desks, distracted. One cocooned in a blanket/hoodie (C19 tent archetype). Paper textbooks open but ignored (C16). A phone visible. Teacher at front looking tired.
**Camera:** Slow dolly-in from rear of classroom.
**Color:** Desaturated, cool gray-blue tint.
**Sound:** Music drops to a single sustained low note.
**Concepts in beat:** C01 (before-state), C16, C19
**Shot #:** 01

### Beat 1.2 — 4eye Arrives (0:03–0:05)
**Action:** Classroom door opens on its own. 4eye character floats in — small, friendly, glowing soft cyan (C03, C24). Warm light follows him into the room.
**Camera:** Cut to medium shot at door, slight low angle to give 4eye presence.
**Color:** First warm spill of the video — cyan-amber glow contrasts with the gray.
**Sound:** Music shifts — soft pulse begins.
**Concepts:** C03, C05, C24
**Shot #:** 02

### Beat 1.3 — The Hand-off (0:05–0:08)
**Action:** 4eye approaches the teacher. With a small gesture, he hands her a glowing translucent device (the HUD). Contact happens. Brief held beat.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, slow push-in toward the contact moment.
**Color:** The cyan-amber spill now centered on the device.
**Sound:** Music begins to swell.
**Concepts:** C03, C05
**Shot #:** 03

### Beat 1.4 — ⭐ HUD Activates (0:08–0:10) — HERO FRAME
**Action:** Teacher lifts the device toward her face. HUD materializes across her field of view, glowing soft cyan + warm amber. Light spills across her eyes and cheeks. Her expression shifts from tired to alert — a held micro-moment. Behind her, slightly out of focus, students begin to lift their heads in unison.
**Camera:** Medium close-up. Brief slow-motion at the activation instant.
**Color:** Peak hero palette — cyan + amber duotone.
**Sound:** Musical hit. Subtle soft pop on activation.
**Concepts:** C01, C03, C05, C07
**Shot #:** 04
**Prompt:** [`../prompts/scene-1-shot-01-hud-reveal.md`](../prompts/scene-1-shot-01-hud-reveal.md)
**Note:** This is the thumbnail, social poster, and website still frame for the entire video. Over-invest here.

### Beat 1.4b — Skill Tree Expansion (≈0:10–0:11)
**Action:** Cut to a single student in profile / three-quarter view. A constellation UI expands outward from their head as a web of 7 circular badge nodes, each containing one MUI outlined glyph (Visibility, Hearing, PanTool, Psychology, Shield, Redeem, ArrowUpward). Thin glowing cyan-amber energy lines connect the nodes. Two nodes have circular progress rings partially filled (~40–70 %), with a soft flash at near-completion. The student's face is gently lit by the incoming skill-tree glow — discovery, potential.
**Camera:** Slow push-in on the student's face.
**Color:** Hero cyan-amber continues. Active nodes slightly brighter with a warm-amber pulse ring.
**Sound:** Soft synth bloom + a single ringing chime at the progress-ring flash.
**Concepts:** C01, C02, C03, C21
**Shot #:** 04b (new — `S1-C-new5`)
**Seed:** [`../gallery-app/apps/api/src/seeds/scenes/s1-c-new5-skill-tree.seed.ts`](../gallery-app/apps/api/src/seeds/scenes/s1-c-new5-skill-tree.seed.ts)
**Timeline note:** ~1 s. Beat 1.5 compressed from 3 s → 2 s to absorb this addition (75 s hero total maintained).

### Beat 1.5 — Students Awaken (0:11–0:13)
**Action:** Students sit up, shuffle to seats. 4wing flies in (C04), circles briefly, lands on a desk near the camera. Phone is pocketed. Tent-kid (C19) peeks out from blanket — curious.
**Camera:** Wider classroom shot, parallax — foreground 4wing in focus, students racking through behind.
**Color:** Saturation returns to the room. Daylight feels warmer.
**Sound:** Music opens up, layered strings.
**Concepts:** C01, C04, C05, C19
**Shot #:** 05

### Beat 1.6 — Gear Accumulation (0:13–0:16)
**Action:** Students gain their own HUDs / gear / faint glowing tattoo-style markings on hands and forearms (C21). Two students visibly higher-level (more gear pieces, brighter glow) — already-equipped, already-focused (C02).
**Camera:** Quick montage — 3 close-ups of different students gaining gear, 1:2:3 growth rhythm.
**Color:** Each student's gear has the cyan-amber accent.
**Sound:** Three rhythmic musical beats sync to the three close-ups.
**Concepts:** C02, C21
**Shot #:** 06

### Beat 1.7 — Engagement Signals (0:16–0:19)
**Action:** Fast montage of engagement feedback — a student's eyes widen with a faint lightning glint, a small coin/zap flashes near another (C10), a leaderboard tick in a corner HUD (C18), a faint clock spinning forward (C23).
**Camera:** Tight cuts, ~0.5s each.
**Color:** Pops of bright cyan and gold.
**Sound:** Light percussive zaps, sync to each flash.
**Concepts:** C10, C18, C23
**Shot #:** 07

### Beat 1.8 — Bully → Engage (0:19–0:22)
**Action:** Two students at the back about to scuffle. Both pause as their HUDs activate. They look up, glance at each other, then both turn to engage — choosing the lesson instead (C20). Reframe of fighting → focus.
**Camera:** Medium two-shot, hold on the pause moment.
**Color:** Brief red flush on the about-to-fight beat, dissolves to cyan as HUDs activate.
**Sound:** Tension chord resolves into the main theme.
**Concepts:** C20
**Shot #:** 08

### Beat 1.9 — Out to Lens 2 (0:22–0:24)
**Action:** Camera pushes forward toward one student's face. HUD geometry expands — triangular shapes form, wipe outward across frame. Transition into Lens 2.
**Camera:** Push-in into HUD overlay.
**Sound:** Whoosh into the lens.
**Concepts:** C09
**Shot #:** 09 (technically the lens transition shot)
