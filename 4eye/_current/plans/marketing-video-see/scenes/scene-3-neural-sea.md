# Scene 3 — Neural / Sea: Dream

**Duration target:**
- 75s master: ~25s (0:42–1:07)
- 30s cut: ~6s (compressed to one aspirational beat)
- 15s cut: 1 shot only (Beat 3.2 or 3.5)
- 6s hook: skip

**Scene promise:** What growth makes possible. Aspirational, dreamlike, expansive. The "future you" payoff. This is the only scene where embedded / internal mods (neural, glowing implants) are allowed — clearly coded as dream / aspiration.

**Primary concepts:** C02 Growth, C13 Water/ocean, C15 Neural dream-state, C21 Gear (elegant evolved form)
**Secondary:** C07 Aspiration, C17 Translation/sharing, C22 Guardian eye
**Tertiary:** C11 Communication, C12 Global, C16 Old paper → instant info, C25 Protect

**Brand themes carried:** Improve, Innovate, Win, Heal, Protect (all five — this is the payoff scene)

**Setting:** Surreal aspirational dreamscape — vast bioluminescent ocean horizon, soft cyan-teal sky, weightless feel, subtle floating geometric particles, sense of expansion and possibility.

**Style anchors:** see `../00-style-bible.md` § 7.5

## Beats (sketch — to expand)

### Beat 3.1 — Bioluminescent Sea Opening (0:42–0:47)
Wide opening — figure on a shore or floating platform, ocean glowing with subtle blue-cyan bioluminescence. Sense of awe.

### Beat 3.2 — Evolved Gear (0:47–0:52)
Character wearing elegant evolved HUD — sleeker, more integrated than Scene 1's version. Still worn, but more refined (C21, C02). Visible leveling progression from Scene 1.

### Beat 3.3 — Neural Sharing (0:52–0:57)
Two characters touch fingertips — light flows between them, glyphs and brief multilingual text bursts appear (C15, C17). Translation / shared knowledge implied without explanation.

### Beat 3.4 — Guardian Eye (0:57–1:02)
Camera pulls back to reveal the world from above — a soft, benevolent 4eye motif in the sky (C22, C12). NOT surveillance — guardian. Quick, then continue.

### Beat 3.5 — Aspirational Ascent (1:02–1:07)
Character rises — HUD in its final evolved form, gear glowing softly. This is the "who you can become" moment (C07, C02).

### Beat 3.6 — Out to Lens 4 (1:07–1:10)
Light from the character's HUD blooms outward, forming the halo / circle-leveled lens shape.

## Critical Constraints
- **This is the ONLY scene** where internal/embedded mods are allowed (per Locked Decision D5)
- Even here, treat them as elegant and dreamlike — never surgical, never invasive
- "Neural" reads as light-flow between people, not as wires-plugged-in
- "Sea" carries the brand water/future motif

## Open Questions
- How surreal is Scene 3? Recommend lean into surreal — this is the dream payoff. Viewer should feel they've moved from "now" to "what could be."
- Show character's face in Beat 3.5, or back-to-camera ascending? Recommend back-to-camera — viewer projects themselves into the role.
- Guardian eye (Beat 3.4): is this 4eye character literally in the sky, or a stylized motif? Recommend stylized — too literal feels heavy-handed.
