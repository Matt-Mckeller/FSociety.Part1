# Guiding Principles

*Foundational values that inform product, business, and strategic decisions across all organizations.*

**Last Updated**: October 29, 2025

## Purpose & Usage

**Purpose**: These principles provide specific decision-making guidance as a third-tier expansion of PURPOSE_AND_GOALS.md and PURPOSE_DATA.md.

**Usage**: Reference when designing features, making business decisions, or evaluating strategic options. Different principles have varying weights depending on audience, business context, and goals.

**Hierarchy**:
1. [`PURPOSE_AND_GOALS.md`](./PURPOSE_AND_GOALS.md) - Highest level vision and goals
2. [`PURPOSE_DATA.md`](./PURPOSE_DATA.md) - Core and secondary themes

## User Experience & Engagement

- **Instant Gratification / Quicker Gratification**: Design for immediate feedback and visible progress
- **Customization/Personalization**: Adapt to individual needs, preferences, and contexts
- **Self-paced experiences**: Users control their timeline and progression
- **Tiered access and upgrades**: Progressive value delivery with clear advancement paths

---

## Achievement & Excellence

- **Being the Best, Winning**: Support competitive excellence and measurable achievement
- **Unlocking Potential**: Help users discover and develop their capabilities
- **Anyone can be a Hero/Superhuman**: Enable transformation and empowerment for all

---

## Purpose & Self-Discovery

- **Teaching humans our "why"**: Help people understand their purpose and identity
- **Learning who we are, who we want to be**: Support self-discovery and identity development
- **Understanding what makes us happy**: Education about emotions, drivers, and fulfillment
- **Understanding how we learn**: Meta-learning and learning science awareness
- **Emphasis on "Why"**: Purpose-driven decision-making at all levels

---

## Access & Opportunity

- **Affordable quality self-paced education**: Quality shouldn't require wealth
- **Equal Opportunity**: Remove systemic barriers to success
- **Building inspirational, world-changing solutions**: Aim for massive positive impact

---

## Excellence in Execution

- **Optimal Implementations / Perfection**: Pursue excellence within real constraints (time, money, goals, context)
- **Context-aware quality**: Balance perfection with practical considerations

## Application Guidelines

### Context Matters

These principles have different weights and priorities depending on:

- **Audience**: Students, teachers, institutions, parents, underprivileged communities, global users
- **Business**: 4eye (learning), 1game (gamification), expanse-work (productivity), 4up (marketing), expanse-services (marketplace)
- **Feature Type**: Core features vs extended features vs future enhancements
- **Geography**: Cultural context and regional needs
- **Development Phase**: MVP vs growth vs enterprise scale

### Not All Equal

Use relevant principles for each decision rather than trying to apply all simultaneously. Some decisions may prioritize user experience principles, while others focus on access and opportunity.

### Decision Framework

1. **Identify**: What decision or design challenge are you facing?
2. **Consider**: Which principles apply to this specific situation?
3. **Weight**: Adjust principle importance based on context (audience, business, goals)
4. **Decide**: Make the choice that best serves the applicable principles
5. **Document**: Note which principles guided the decision (especially for major choices)

## Examples of Application

### Example 1: Feature Prioritization (4eye)
**Decision**: Should we build advanced gamification or more AI tutoring features first?

**Relevant Principles**:
- Instant Gratification (gamification provides immediate rewards)
- Unlocking Potential (AI tutoring helps students discover capabilities)
- Building inspirational solutions (both contribute)

**Context**: K-12 students struggle with engagement more than access to information

**Decision**: Prioritize gamification (coins, XP) in Tier 1 because engagement is the primary barrier for target audience

---

### Example 2: Pricing Strategy (4eye)
**Decision**: What should be free vs paid?

**Relevant Principles**:
- Affordable quality education (keep core learning free)
- Equal Opportunity (don't gate essential features behind paywall)
- Tiered access (provide upgrade path)

**Context**: Global audience, including underprivileged communities

**Decision**: Content free globally, some features available for free ( i.e. no visuals ); premium features (advanced analytics, additional customization) paid

---

### Example 3: Technical Architecture (All Businesses)
**Decision**: Start with microservices or monolith?

**Relevant Principles**:
- Optimal Implementations / Perfection (within constraints)
- Building world-changing solutions (need to launch and validate)

**Context**: Limited resources, need to validate product-market fit

**Decision**: Start with monolith (Phase 1), evolve to microservices (Phase 3) - excellence within real constraints

## Notes

- These principles are living guidelines, not rigid rules
- When principles conflict, use context and business goals to prioritize
- Document major decisions and which principles influenced them
- Review and update these principles quarterly as businesses evolve
- Share examples of principle application to build organizational understanding
