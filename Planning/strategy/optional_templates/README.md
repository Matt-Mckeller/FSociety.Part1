# Strategy

**Purpose:** Define your north star, values, and how you measure success.

This directory contains your strategic foundation - the "What" and "Why" that guides all your planning and decision-making.

## Files

### `vision.md`
Your 3-5 year vision - where are you headed? What does success look like?

**Update:** Quarterly or when major life/business changes occur

### `values.md`
Core values and decision-making principles that guide your choices.

**Update:** Annually or when you discover new values that resonate

### `annual-themes.md`
Year-level focus areas - the 2-3 themes that define this year's priorities.

**Update:** Annually at year planning, review quarterly

### `success-metrics.md`
How you define and measure success across different areas of life/work.

**Update:** Quarterly to adjust metrics based on learnings

---

## How to Use

1. **Start here when beginning a new year** - Define your themes and vision
2. **Reference when making big decisions** - Does this align with your values?
3. **Review during quarterly planning** - Are you still on track with your vision?
4. **Use to validate roadmap goals** - Do your quarterly goals support your annual themes?

---

## Integration with Other Directories

- **→ Roadmap:** Annual themes become quarterly objectives
- **→ Alignment:** Health checks validate strategic alignment
- **→ Reflection:** Reviews check if you're living your values
- **→ Execution:** Projects should link to strategic goals

---

*These files are the foundation of your entire planning system. Everything else cascades from here.*
