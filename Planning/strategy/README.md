# Strategy

**Purpose**: Strategic foundation for all businesses and projects.

## What's In Here

### Vision & Values
- `vision-and-values.md` - Long-term vision, personal goals, core values *(To be created in Phase 1C)*
- `success-metrics.md` - How success is measured across businesses *(To be created in Phase 1C)*

### Brand & Design
- `brand-and-design-system/` - Brand identity, design principles, content examples shared across all businesses

### Business Strategy
- `business-strategy/` - How businesses work together, shared resources, synergies *(To be populated in Phase 1C and 1E)*

## How This Differs From Roadmap

- **Strategy** = The "why" and "what" (vision, values, goals)
- **Roadmap** = The "when" and "how" (timeline, milestones, priorities)

Strategy is more stable and changes less frequently. Roadmap is tactical and updated regularly.

## How This Differs From Individual Business Docs

- **Strategy** = Cross-business, umbrella level, applies to all
- **Business Docs** = Specific to one business

If it applies to all businesses, it goes here. If it's specific to one business, it goes in that business's folder.
