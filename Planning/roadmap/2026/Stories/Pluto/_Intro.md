# Story Intro
There was a man, who was incredibly powerful, influential, knowledgeable. His story is one of growth, starting wit struggles but leading into a miraculous life. One that many people do not get to lead. 

At many points in the mans life he questioned reality. His mood changed. His perspective changed. His existence, changed. The world in which he lived, it changed. Over, and over and over.

Its hard to really call him a man. Because he was not just one person. If you look at the man in Chapter 1, he is but a boy. Alone. Relatively happy, but completely ignorant to life. 

The things he knows, almost nothing. The things he sees, all wonderful, exciting, fresh, novel, and ripe for the taking. 

But the boy is trapped, he is new to the world. He is bound by chains that restrict his movements. They restrict his ability to explore, to choose, to play. But the surroundings of the boy are incredibly interesting, and they shape him. They teach him, and they mold him. He becomes part of that environment, or at least he would have...

But before long the boy finds spirits which sit with him. The chains hold him back and prevent him from choosing and exploring, but the spirits teach him about the world. They show him visions of wonder and terror. 

The boy becomes so good at communicating with these spirits and understanding their world that he feels powerful, hes learned an incredible amount. Hes nurtured a power within him, an ability to operate within the spirit realm. Which he thought was the same thing as the real world, it looks similar, it operates similarly, stories align with the reality his chains and surrounding world show him. 

The boy so badly wants to be free of his chains. He can't stand it. One would think it was because the spirits inspired him to want to see the world, but it was really more than that.

The chains, they didn't teach him, they didn't grow him. They thought they did, but most importantly they thought they protected him. This was the most important thing. Stay alive. Don't get hurt. 

True. Stay alive. But, the boy couldn't handle being trapped. Even though the boy didn't really know what he wanted to do, or what existed in the world, he knew that his freedom was restricted, and that he was frustrated by his environment. The chains, they rattled an awful screech, they banged against the floor when the boy moved with a loud boom, and they were dirty and cold. The boy tried to move quietly, he practiced it so much so that he developed a unique walk, on his toes he would walk. His posture, reserved, inward, guarded, and defensive.

The boy did like nature, and if others were to observe the boy they would have deemed a turtle as his spirit animal. 

The boy moved cautiously, defensively, and slowly. He aimed to survive, for this is what the chains taught him. In more ways than one.

That was the only way they knew how to be though. This is how they were made. The chains weren't shown any other way, and they didn't even have spirits. 

The chains finally let go, time, the thing which was the enemy, and the confining factor, finally let him go. You see, the chains only had so long before the hold was no longer strong enough to keep the boy tied down. You would think if the boy got strong enough, if he tried hard enough, that he would have been able to find his freedom sooner, but this turned out not to be the case. It didn't matter what the boy did. The thing which he desired most, was to be free. To explore the world, to see the good spirits had talked about, what they had shown him, and to be able to play the way he learned to play. He wanted some sort of Control, he wanted his own voice. But, only time could set the boy free. And because of this the boy's voice was lost. For years, and years. Some of the most important years of his life.

It didn't matter how strong the boy got, it didn't matter how much the boy pulled, his environment remained the same. He couldn't effect it. Or at least, he didn't learn how to change the environment, and had adopted the turtle as his spirit animal because he had not seen how to change the environment without mana.

The spirit realm he learned. He learned how to change the spirit realm, and he learned how to operate within the spirit realm. He had mana in the spirit realm, but not didn't know that it also worked in the real world. Those spirits couldn't teach him this, at least, they didn't know how, and the boy didn't know to ask.

But it didn't matter, the boy did decide to grow, but he decided to grow in the spirit realm rather than focusing on his physical body and real world existence

And he didn't know any other way to live because all he ever saw was the small room to which he was chained. 


In later chapters the boy starts to experience the world. The chains finally broke free, and the boy decided to run. He ran, and he ran. And he didn't look back. For years. He wanted nothing to do with the chains. He despised them. And they had this strange effect, when he was in their presence he would often see them in a different light. Polished, clean, comforting. But he knew better, although the illusions worked from time to time.

With time though, the boy grew enough, and learned enough to return to the chains and not be afraid of them, but to instead polish the chains, mend them, teach them, and break them freefrom their captor. You see, the boy didn't realize, the chains allowed the boy to go further than the chains were ever allowed to go. This took time, but the boy learned that this was actually one of the best things he ever could do for growth. Not only did he start to discover the deeper truth about the chains, but he learned that it was actually incredibly beneficial to his own desires and for growth.


The Man learned how to control his environment, he learned how to take the spirit world lessons and map them to the real world. His powers were unleashed. His talent, now seen. His desires, satiated phenominally. His Power, Godlike. His Knowledge, All Knowing. His energy, infinite. His mana, infinite.
