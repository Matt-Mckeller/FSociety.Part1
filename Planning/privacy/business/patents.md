## Key Concepts

### Independent Patent:

Your invention stands alone
Doesn't require someone else's patented technology
You can make and sell freely (once granted)

### Dependent Patent:

Your invention builds on or incorporates someone else's patented technology
You CAN get a patent for your innovation
You CANNOT manufacture without licensing the underlying patent
