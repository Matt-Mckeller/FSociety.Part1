# Keyboard RF Emissions Explained 🎹⚡
What Are Keyboard RF Emissions?
Definition: When you press a key on your keyboard, it creates small electromagnetic (radio frequency) signals that can be detected and decoded from a distance.

How It Works:
// Physical process when you type
1. Key Press:
   [Finger] → [Key] → [Switch closes]
   
2. Electrical Signal:
   Switch creates voltage change in keyboard circuit
   Current flows through traces/wires
   
3. Unintentional RF Emission:
   Current in wire = tiny antenna
   Radiates EM waves (10-500 MHz typically)
   
4. Each Key Has Unique Signal:
   Different keys = different electrical paths
   Different timing/waveforms
   Can be "fingerprinted"

5. Attack:
   Attacker captures RF signals (radio receiver)
   Analyzes waveforms (ML/signal processing)
   Reconstructs what you typed
