# The EM spectrum (low → high frequency)
Radio: kHz–GHz (kilometers–meters)
Microwaves: GHz (centimeters)
Infrared: 0.7–300 µm
Visible: ~400–700 nm (violet → red)
Ultraviolet: ~10–400 nm
X‑rays: ~0.01–10 nm
Gamma rays: <0.01 nm
X‑rays and gamma rays are still “light” (EM), but they’re high‑energy, ionizing bands; visible is non‑ionizing.
