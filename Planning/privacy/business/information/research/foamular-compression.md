## Foamular compression
Standard Foamular 150/250/400/600:

At 10% compression: 15-60 PSI (depends on density)
At 5% compression: 10-40 PSI
For your 2" thick foam:

Foamular 150: ~15 PSI at 10% deformation
Foamular 250: ~25 PSI at 10% deformation
Foamular 400: ~40 PSI at 10% deformation
Foamular 600: ~60 PSI at 10% deformation
What does 10% deformation mean?

On 2" foam: 0.2 inches of compression before it starts to permanently deform
On 0.5" foam: 0.05 inches (1.3mm) compression

## The Physics: Why Steel Amplifies Magnetic Force: Magnetic Flux Concentration Effect:

WITHOUT Steel Backing (field spreads equally):
        ↑ ↑ ↑
      ↗  ↑  ↖       Field spreads 360° (wasted)

  [N - Magnet - S]
    ↖    ↓    ↗     50% of field goes backward
      ↖  ↓  ↗

        ↑↑↑↑↑↑↑       Field concentrated forward!
