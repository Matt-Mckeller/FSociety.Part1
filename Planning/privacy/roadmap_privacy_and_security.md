### What Frequencies Are Protected (Detailed):

**✅ FULLY PROTECTED (70dB = 99.99999% attenuation):**
- VHF (30-300 MHz) - TV, FM radio, some radar
- UHF (300 MHz - 3 GHz) - Cell phones, TV, WiFi, GPS, radar
- SHF (3-30 GHz) - WiFi 5GHz, satellite, advanced radar
- EHF (30-40 GHz) - 5G, mmWave
- **TEMPEST emissions** (10 MHz - 1 GHz) - Screen content reconstruction
- **Wireless keyboards** (2.4 GHz) - Keystroke interception
- **WiFi** (2.4/5 GHz) - Network traffic, positioning
- **Bluetooth** (2.4 GHz) - Device tracking, sniffing
- **Cell signals** (700 MHz - 2.6 GHz) - Location tracking
- **GPS** (1.5 GHz) - Position tracking

**⚠️ PARTIALLY PROTECTED:**
- Thermal infrared (70-85% reduction, not 100%)
- Frequencies below 10 MHz (but irrelevant for password security)
- Frequencies above 40 GHz (rare advanced threats)

**❌ NOT PROTECTED:**
- Acoustic (keystroke sounds) → Need white noise generator
- Seismic (vibrations through floor) → Minor threat
- Very low frequencies <10 MHz → Not relevant for keystroke detection

- ✅ Visual satellites: BLOCKED (100% blackout)
- ✅ Thermal satellites: REDUCED (70-85% signature reduction)
- ✅ SIGINT satellites: BLOCKED (70dB RF attenuation covers all RF bands)
- ✅ GPS tracking: BLOCKED

**Drones:**
