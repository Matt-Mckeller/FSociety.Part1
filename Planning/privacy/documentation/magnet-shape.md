# Circle vs. Rectangle Magnetic Fields

Rectangle = more powerful towards the ends = closer to the point of contact

## Comparison Table

| Feature | Circle Magnet | Rectangle Magnet |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **Magnetic Field** | More uniform and consistent. | Concentrated on the ends with a strong, stable grip. |
| **Strength** | Can be very strong, especially in cylindrical or disc shapes. | Strong grip and stable due to large surface contact area. |
| **Best For** | Rotating machinery (like motors and sensors) due to easier rotation and a consistent field. | Mounting, displays, and holding applications where stability and friction are key. |
| **Assembly** | Designed to be embedded in circular configurations. | Easier to install due to flat surfaces and straight edges. |

## Why the Difference in Magnetic Fields?

**Shape and contact area:** A rectangular shape provides a greater surface area for contact, which leads to a stronger and more stable hold. A circular shape, on the other hand, is more efficient for applications requiring rotation or a consistent magnetic field around a central axis.
