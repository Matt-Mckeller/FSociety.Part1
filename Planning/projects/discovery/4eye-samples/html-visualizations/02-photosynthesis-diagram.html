📚 Understanding the Process

1️⃣ Light-Dependent Reactions

Location: Thylakoid membranes

Requirements:

  • ☀️ Light energy
  • 💧 Water (H₂O)

Products:

  • 🔋 ATP (energy)
  • 🔋 NADPH (energy carrier)
  • 🌬️ Oxygen (O₂) - released!

The chloroplast captures sunlight and splits water molecules!

2️⃣ Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent)

Location: Stroma

Requirements:

  • 💨 Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
  • 🔋 ATP (from stage 1)
  • 🔋 NADPH (from stage 1)

Products:

  • 🍬 Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) - sugar!

Uses energy to build sugar from CO₂ - works day and night!

💡 Key Insight

Why Two Stages? Stage 1 captures light energy and converts it into chemical energy (ATP/NADPH). Stage 2 uses that chemical energy to build glucose from CO₂. Think of it like a two-step factory: first charge the batteries (Stage 1), then use those batteries to build products (Stage 2)!

🌍 Why It Matters

Photosynthesis is the foundation of nearly all life on Earth! Plants create the oxygen we breathe and the food we eat. When you eat plants (or animals that ate plants), you're eating captured sunlight. That's why we say photosynthesis is the most important chemical reaction on the planet! 🌱
