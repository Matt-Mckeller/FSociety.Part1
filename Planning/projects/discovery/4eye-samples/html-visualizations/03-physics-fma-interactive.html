💡 Real-World Example

🎯 What is Force?

Force is a push or pull measured in Newtons (N).

  • More force = more push
  • 10 N is like pushing with moderate strength
  • 100 N is like pushing very hard

⚖️ What is Mass?

Mass is the amount of matter in an object, measured in kilograms (kg).

  • More mass = heavier object
  • 5 kg is like a small bag of flour
  • 50 kg is like a large dog

🏃 What is Acceleration?

Acceleration is how quickly speed changes, measured in meters per second squared (m/s²).

  • Higher = speeds up faster
  • 2 m/s² = moderate acceleration
  • 10 m/s² ≈ gravity on Earth
