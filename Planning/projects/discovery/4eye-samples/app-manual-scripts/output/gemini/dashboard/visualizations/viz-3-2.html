Domains of Inquiry: Science and Faith

SVG_DIAGRAM medium effectiveness
Visualization Description
This visualization directly supports the teacher's introductory statement about reconciling science and faith. It can help students compartmentalize the different ways of knowing and understand the specific scope of the science classroom.
Generation Prompt
Create an SVG diagram with two large, overlapping circles (a Venn diagram). Label the left circle 'Science' and the right circle 'Faith/Philosophy'. Inside the 'Science' circle, list keywords: 'Empirical Evidence', 'Testable Hypotheses', 'How?', 'Natural World'. Inside the 'Faith/Philosophy' circle, list keywords: 'Meaning & Purpose', 'Values & Morality', 'Why?', 'Supernatural'. In the small overlapping section, write 'Human Experience', 'Wonder', 'Ethics'. Add a title at the top: 'Different Questions, Different Tools'. Underneath the diagram, add an annotation: 'In this class, we are using the tools of science to answer scientific questions.'
