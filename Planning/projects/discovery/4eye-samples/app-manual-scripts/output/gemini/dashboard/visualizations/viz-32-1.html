How to Solve by Factoring

graph TD' orientation for a 9th-grade algebra class. The title should be 'Solving x² + 5x + 6 = 0'. Start node: A['Start: x² + 5x + 6 = 0']. Next step: B{'Find two numbers that multiply to 6 AND add to 5'}. Next: C['The numbers are 2 and 3']. Next: D['Rewrite as factors: (x+2)(x+3) = 0']. Next: E{'Set each factor to zero'}. Create two parallel paths from E: F['x+2=0'] --> G['x = -2'] and H['x+3=0'] --> I['x = -3']. Final node: J['Solutions: x=-2, x=-3']. Use rounded rectangles for process steps and diamond shapes for decisions/questions.
Why this visualization helps learning:
A flowchart breaks down the multi-step process of factoring into a simple, visual algorithm. This helps students who struggle with procedural memory and provides a clear, repeatable guide for solving similar problems.
