What is a 'Scientific Theory'?

INFOGRAPHIC very_high effectiveness
Visualization Description
This directly addresses a common misconception that 'theory' means 'guess.' Clarifying this at the start is critical for student understanding and reduces potential resistance to the topic. It establishes the robust, evidence-based nature of a scientific theory.
Generation Prompt
Create a simple, clear infographic for a 9th-grade biology class titled 'What is a Scientific Theory?'. Use two columns. Left column titled 'In Everyday Language' with an icon of people talking, and text: 'An idea or a hunch. Example: My theory is that the dog stole the cookies.' Right column titled 'In Science' with an icon of a microscope or DNA helix, and text: 'A well-substantiated explanation of the natural world, based on a body of facts that have been repeatedly confirmed through observation and experiment.' Below the columns, have a summary box: 'A scientific theory isn't a guess—it's the strongest explanation we have!' Use a clean, modern font and a simple color palette (e.g., blue and green). Ensure high contrast for readability.
