Aerobic Respiration vs. Fermentation

COMPARISON_MATRIX medium effectiveness
Visualization Description
A direct comparison table helps students organize the key differences between aerobic and anaerobic pathways, which is a common point of confusion. It highlights the vast difference in energy efficiency.
Generation Prompt
Create a clean, two-column HTML table comparing 'Aerobic Respiration' and 'Anaerobic Fermentation'. The rows should be: 'Main Goal', 'Oxygen Required?', 'Location(s) in Cell', 'Net ATP Yield per Glucose', 'Key Stages Involved', and 'Final Products'. For Aerobic Respiration, the data should be: 'Complete glucose breakdown for max ATP', 'Yes', 'Cytoplasm & Mitochondria', '~32 ATP', 'Glycolysis, Krebs Cycle, ETC', 'CO₂, H₂O, ATP'. For Anaerobic Fermentation, the data should be: 'Regenerate NAD+ to continue glycolysis', 'No', 'Cytoplasm only', '2 ATP', 'Glycolysis & Fermentation pathways', 'Lactic Acid (animals) or Ethanol + CO₂ (yeast)'. Use clear headings and alternating row colors for readability.
