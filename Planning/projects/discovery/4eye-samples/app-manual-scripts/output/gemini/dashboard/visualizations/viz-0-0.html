The Holocaust in the Context of World War II (1933-1945)

TIMELINE_COMPARATIVE very_high effectiveness
Visualization Description
A comparative timeline is crucial for understanding that the Holocaust was not an isolated event, but a systematic process that escalated alongside the major military and political events of World War II. It provides critical context and shows the progression from discrimination to genocide.
Generation Prompt
Create an interactive HTML comparative timeline for 10th-grade history students covering 1933-1945. Create two parallel tracks. The top track, 'World War II Major Events,' should include: Hitler becomes Chancellor (1933), Invasion of Poland (1939), Invasion of USSR (1941), Pearl Harbor (1941), D-Day (1944), V-E Day (1945). The bottom track, 'Escalation of the Holocaust,' should include: Nuremberg Laws (1935), Kristallnacht (1938), Establishment of Ghettos (1939-), Wannsee Conference (1942), Liberation of Camps (1945). Use a somber, respectful color palette (grays, dark blues). On hover, display a brief (1-2 sentence) description of each event. Draw subtle connecting lines to show relationships, e.g., from 'Invasion of Poland' to 'Establishment of Ghettos'.
