The Pillars of Consent

INFOGRAPHIC high effectiveness
Visualization Description
An infographic can visually summarize the key components of consent in a memorable and easily digestible format. Using an acronym like FRIES (Freely Given, Reversible, Informed, Enthusiastic, Specific) provides a powerful mnemonic device.
Generation Prompt
Create a vertical infographic for middle/high school students titled 'Consent is as easy as FRIES'. Use a friendly, modern design with a clear icon for each letter. F - Freely Given: Icon of a hand offering a gift. Text: 'Consent is a choice, not a response to pressure or guilt.' R - Reversible: Icon of a refresh/undo arrow. Text: 'Anyone can change their mind at any time.' I - Informed: Icon of a lightbulb. Text: 'You know exactly what you are agreeing to.' E - Enthusiastic: Icon of a smiling face or thumbs-up. Text: 'It should be a wholehearted YES, not an unsure maybe.' S - Specific: Icon of a target. Text: 'Saying yes to one thing doesn't mean you've said yes to anything else.' Use a distinct color for each section.
