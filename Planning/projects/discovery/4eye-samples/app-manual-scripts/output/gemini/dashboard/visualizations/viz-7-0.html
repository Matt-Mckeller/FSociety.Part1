The Consent Checklist

DECISION_TREE very_high effectiveness
Visualization Description
A decision tree or flowchart provides a clear, logical, and visual way to process the abstract concept of consent. It breaks down the decision-making process into simple yes/no questions, making it easy for students to understand and apply.
Generation Prompt
Create a Mermaid flowchart for a 9th-grade health class titled 'The Consent Checklist'. Start with a box: 'Do you want to do something with someone?'. Arrow to a decision diamond: 'Have you asked them clearly?'. 'No' arrow leads to 'Stop. Ask first!'. 'Yes' arrow leads to another diamond: 'Did they give a clear, enthusiastic YES?'. 'No' or 'Unsure' arrow leads to 'Stop. The answer is NO.'. 'Yes' arrow leads to a final box: 'Great! Enjoy the activity respectfully and remember they can change their mind at any time.'. Use green for 'Yes' paths and red for 'No' paths. Use simple, friendly icons next to each box (e.g., a question mark for asking, a checkmark for yes, a stop sign for no).
