Visualization Description
A flowchart provides a clear, unambiguous, step-by-step guide for a high-stakes decision. It reduces cognitive load in a stressful situation and reinforces the correct, safe action.
Generation Prompt
Create a simple and clear Mermaid flowchart for middle or high school students titled 'How to Help a Friend in Crisis'. Use a supportive and calm color palette (e.g., soft blues and greens). Start node: 'A friend tells you they are struggling'. Diamond decision point: 'Do they mention hurting themselves?'. 'No' path leads to a box: 'Listen, show you care, and encourage them to talk to an adult'. 'Yes' path leads to a red-bordered, high-emphasis box: 'TELL A TRUSTED ADULT IMMEDIATELY. This is not a secret to be kept.' From the 'Yes' box, an arrow points to a final box: 'You can also share the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or Text 988 anytime.' Ensure the font is large, clear, and easy to read. The 'Yes' path should be visually distinct and direct.
