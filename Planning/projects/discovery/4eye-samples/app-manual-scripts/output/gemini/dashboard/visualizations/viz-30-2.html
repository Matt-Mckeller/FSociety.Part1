ANIMATED_SVG high effectiveness
Visualization Description
The core mechanism of *how* these gases cause warming was mentioned but not explained. A simple animation can visually demonstrate the process of heat being trapped, which is a difficult concept to grasp with text alone.
Generation Prompt
Create a simple, looping animated SVG to explain the greenhouse effect. Step 1: Show yellow sun rays traveling from the sun to the Earth. Step 2: Show most rays bouncing off the Earth and going back into space. Step 3: Show a thin 'atmosphere' layer. Step 4: Add CO2 and CH4 molecules to the atmosphere layer. Step 5: Now, when the rays bounce off the Earth, show some of them being trapped by the CO2/CH4 molecules and bouncing back down to Earth, with a small red 'warming' glow on the surface. Use simple shapes and clear, slow animations suitable for a science lesson.
