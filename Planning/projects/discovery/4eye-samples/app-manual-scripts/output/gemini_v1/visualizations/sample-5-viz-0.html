🔬Understanding the Process

📥INPUTS (What Goes In)

  • Sunlight: Provides the energy needed to power the entire process
  • Carbon Dioxide (CO₂): A gas from the air that enters through tiny holes in leaves called stomata
  • Water (H₂O): Absorbed by roots from the soil and travels up the stem to the leaves

⚗️THE MAGIC HAPPENS

  • All three inputs meet inside special parts of the leaf called chloroplasts
  • These chloroplasts contain chlorophyll, which makes plants green and captures sunlight
  • The sunlight's energy converts CO₂ and H₂O into new substances through a chemical reaction

📤OUTPUTS (What Comes Out)

  • Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆): A type of sugar that the plant uses as food and energy to grow
  • Oxygen (O₂): Released into the air as a byproduct - this is the oxygen we breathe!

🌍WHY IT MATTERS

  • Photosynthesis is the foundation of life on Earth
  • Every breath of oxygen you take was made by a plant or algae
  • All food (plants, and animals that eat plants) starts with photosynthesis
  • Plants help fight climate change by removing CO₂ from the atmosphere
  • Forests, oceans, and even your houseplants are tiny factories making life possible!

🤔 Fun Fact!

The word "photosynthesis" comes from Greek: "photo" means light and "synthesis" means putting together. So photosynthesis literally means "putting together with light"!
