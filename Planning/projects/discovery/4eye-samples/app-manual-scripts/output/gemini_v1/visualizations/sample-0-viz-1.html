Understanding the Chain of Events

This flowchart illustrates how each major action by the British government led to a specific reaction from the American colonists, creating a chain reaction that ultimately resulted in war.

The Pattern: British policies → Colonial protest → British punishment → Colonial unity → Armed resistance

Notice how instead of subduing the colonies, harsh British responses actually strengthened colonial resolve and unity, leading inevitably to open conflict.
