Sunlight (Energy)

The sun provides the energy that powers photosynthesis! Without sunlight, plants wouldn't be able to make their food. The light energy is captured by chlorophyll in the plant's leaves.

×
💨

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

A gas from the air that plants use to make food. Plants absorb carbon dioxide through tiny holes in their leaves called stomata. This is one of the main ingredients for making glucose!

×
💧

Water (H₂O)

Water is absorbed from the soil through the plant's roots. It travels up through the stem to the leaves where it's used in photosynthesis. Water is essential for creating glucose and oxygen!

×
🍃

Oxygen (O₂)

Oxygen is released by plants as a byproduct of photosynthesis. This is the same oxygen we breathe! One reason why plants are so important is they clean the air and give us oxygen to survive.

×
🍬

Glucose (Sugar/Food)

Glucose is a type of sugar that plants create during photosynthesis. It's their food! Plants use this energy to grow, make flowers, and produce fruit. Some glucose is stored for later use.
