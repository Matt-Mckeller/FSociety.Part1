🌱 The Process of Photosynthesis 🌱

How Plants Make Their Own Food!

🌟 How Photosynthesis Works

Plants are amazing! They take in carbon dioxide (CO₂) from the air through tiny holes in their leaves, and water (H₂O) through their roots. Using energy from sunlight, they convert these into glucose (sugar), which is their food. As a byproduct, they release oxygen (O₂) that we breathe. That's why plants are so important for life on Earth!

☀️ Sunlight (Energy)
Provides the energy needed for the process
💨 Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)
A gas from the air absorbed by leaves
💧 Water (H₂O)
Absorbed from the soil through roots
🍃 Oxygen (O₂)
Released as a byproduct we can breathe
🍬 Glucose (Sugar)
The food/energy plants create for themselves
