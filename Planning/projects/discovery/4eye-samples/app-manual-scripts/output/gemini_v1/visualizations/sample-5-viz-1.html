🔄 The Animation Sequence

1. Sunlight rays hit the leaf from above
2. Carbon dioxide (CO₂) molecules float from the air into the leaf
3. Water (H₂O) travels up from the roots through the stem
4. The leaf glows green as the magic happens inside
5. Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) is created and stays in the leaf
6. Oxygen (O₂) is released back into the air

Watch closely as the animation loops to see the full process!
