🔆 Sensory Memory

Duration: Less than 1 second

Function: Holds raw sensory information from our environment

Types:
• Iconic: Visual sensory memory
• Echoic: Auditory sensory memory

This allows us to perceive the world as continuous rather than discrete snapshots.

💭 Short-Term / Working Memory

Capacity: ~4 chunks of information (Miller's research: 7±2)

Duration: 20-30 seconds without rehearsal

Function: Temporary storage while actively processing information

Crucial for reasoning, comprehension, and learning. This is your mental "workspace" where you manipulate information.

📚 Long-Term Memory

Capacity: Virtually unlimited

Duration: Years to a lifetime

Function: Permanent storage of experiences, facts, and skills

Two main categories:
• Explicit (Declarative): Conscious memories you can describe
• Implicit (Non-Declarative): Unconscious memories like skills

The hippocampus plays a critical role in forming new explicit memories.

🔬 Case Study: Patient H.M.

Background: Patient H.M. had his hippocampus removed during surgery.

Result: After the surgery, he could no longer form new long-term explicit memories. He couldn't remember events that happened after his surgery or learn new facts.

Surprising Finding: However, his implicit memory remained intact! He could still learn new motor skills, even though he wouldn't remember practicing them.

What This Teaches Us: This famous case demonstrated that different types of memory are stored in different parts of the brain, and that explicit and implicit memory systems can operate independently.
