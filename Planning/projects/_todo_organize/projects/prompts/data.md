🏷️ 1. Core / Descriptive Tags

Describe what the image is — its subject and context.

Category Example Tags / Keys Purpose
Subject / Content portrait, landscape, architecture, food, animal, product, ui, concept_art Identifies the main theme
Setting / Environment indoor, outdoor, studio, urban, nature, fantasy, sci-fi Defines context or world
Composition Type close_up, wide_shot, macro, flat_lay, aerial, isometric Helps classify framing and structure
Emotion / Mood calm, energetic, mysterious, romantic, gritty, serene Helps with tone and storytelling
Purpose / Use Case marketing, illustration, concept_art, thumbnail, tutorial Ties it to workflow or intent

### 🧩 1. Composition & Framing

Describes how elements are arranged.

- composition – overall layout (balanced, centered, asymmetrical)
- framing – subject placement, rule of thirds, leading lines
- focus – where attention is drawn
- depth – sense of space or layers
- symmetry / asymmetry
- negative_space

✅ Improvement keys: better framing, crop adjustment, subject centering, add depth, remove clutter

### 🎨 2. Color & Lighting

Describes visual tone and exposure.

- color_palette – dominant colors
- contrast
- brightness
- saturation
- lighting_type – natural, studio, backlit, ambient, harsh, soft
- white_balance
- shadows / highlights

✅ Improvement keys: color harmony, exposure correction, reduce saturation, improve lighting direction

### 👤 3. Subject & Semantics

Describes what’s in the image and what it represents.

- subject_type – person, object, scene, abstract
- pose / action
- expression (for people)
- object_count
- context – setting, environment
- semantic_focus – what the image means (e.g. joy, power, loneliness)

✅ Improvement keys: clarify subject, remove distractions, enhance subject prominence, fix pose/expression

### 🏗️ 4. Style & Aesthetic

Describes the artistic or visual style.

- style – realistic, minimal, flat, cinematic
