# Story: 
## Intro Scene(s) / Phase 1
## Scene Details
At a high level this a loop of the same environments and characters but with slight variations. There are 3 iterations in the loop. We tell the story through the changes in the environment over time.
The goals are to:
- Depict the main characters development and awakening to a clearer perspective and finding a missing piece of his life represented by female character 2.
- Represent being unable to clearly see and focus on the girl in the beginning as he’s distracted by other things. But over time he begins to pay more attention to the girl, put in effort, and begin to see who she really is. This can be visually represented by the girl appearing kind of blurred initially then transitioning into a less blurred version in loop iteration 2 and a completely clear and 0 blur perspective in iteration 3.

The loop is described as the following: 
- We are depicting the main characters routine day after day
- We depict the main characters life in 3 different environments on repeat 3 times so a total of 9 short clips.
- We iterate through an environment and a scenes of  
    - 1) The main character at home in his bedroom on a laptop at home
    - 2) The main character walking through the streets to a coffee shop
    - 3) The main character sitting down and drinking coffee before returning home going to sleep alone in a bed


Background
The characters entire existence also seems a bit surreal too, and he’s trying to understand it. It makes him slightly uncomfortable. Perhaps a spiritual aura game vibe if that makes sense. 
He’s also partially distracted by random magical miracles that keep happening around him which is why his reality feels pretty surreal.

Scenery and Visual Elements
The environment is a modern robotic city in the ocean.

Plot Elements
He’s trying to process his reality and feels like he’s waking up to see he’s living in the Matrix or some sort of simulated reality. You can process this as buildings glitching and switching form. People teleporting from spot to spot as if blinking. 

Additional Scene Data
The character has an action bar that floats around him with abilities he can press for various effects. Anime style Projected HUD. He doesn’t use them for some reason, but they’re there.

Walking the Streets Details
The first time the character is seen walking down the street he notices groups of people, all families. Some with kids. Some more business some more casual. Some with kids, some without. The female main character is in the scene but not really visible.

Coffee Shop Details
