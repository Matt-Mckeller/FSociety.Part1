Quick Reactions (Social Bar)

Quick-reaction feedback options that let users signal their understanding and engagement in real time. These appear on the Social Bar and can also surface as floating reactions during presenter mode.

🧠 Understanding Signals
ReactionDescription
😕 I'm Confused
Signals the user doesn't understand — can trigger AI assistance or flag for presenter
🙋 Help Me Understand
Requests additional explanation or alternative framing
✅ I Get It
Confirms comprehension — positive signal to move forward
🐢 Slow Down
Requests the presenter or content to pace slower
🚀 Speed Up
Signals the user is ready for more
💬 Engagement Reactions
ReactionDescription
👍 I Like It
Positive sentiment signal
🌟 Great
Strong positive signal
👍👎 Thumbs Up / Down
Binary feedback
😊 Emoji React
Expressive emoji reactions (configurable set)
✓✗ Agree / Disagree
Opinion signal on the current content
📤 Share
Share the current content or slide
In Presenter Mode, aggregated audience reactions can be displayed to the presenter in real time (e.g., "73% say I Get It, 12% say I'm Confused").

✏️ Correction Suggestion
"Here's the corrected version with explanation"
