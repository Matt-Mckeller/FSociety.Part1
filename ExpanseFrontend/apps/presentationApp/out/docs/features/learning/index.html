Learning features are organized into three layers: the pedagogical strategies that guide how AI teaches, the discrete actions users can trigger, and the UI elements that surface learning interactions.

AI Learning Strategies

These are the pedagogical principles that govern how the AI presents, reinforces, and tests knowledge. They should be surfaced as an interactive UI component — not just hidden background behaviors.

UI Surface: A Learning Strategy Panel (tab-based or action bar) that lets the user select a strategy and see the current content re-presented through that lens.
StrategyDescriptionExample
Active Recall
Forces retrieval of information rather than passive re-reading. Strengthens memory by making the learner reconstruct knowledge
After explaining protein digestion, the AI asks you to restate it in your own words
Desirable Difficulty
Introduces manageable challenges that strengthen retention. Adjustable difficulty setting
"Predict what happens if you skip carbs after training"
Chunking & Layering
Breaks topics into progressive layers from basic → complex. Each layer builds on the previous
Layer 1: Amino acids → Layer 2: Synthesis → Layer 3: Signaling → Layer 4: Optimization
Application Bias
Knowledge sticks better when applied to real scenarios
Simulate a 5-day training schedule with protein timing
Metacognition
Prompts the learner to think about how they learn — identifying what's hard, what's easy, and why
"Which part of this explanation is hardest to remember?"
Retrieval > Re-reading
Actively recalling information strengthens memory more than passively consuming it
Explain → Restate → Expand cycle

Content Pipelines & Transformations

Moved to Marketing: Content pipelines (Perspective, Emotional, Engagement) are now documented in the Marketing → Content → Pipelines section, as they relate to content strategy and personalization.

Content pipelines are deeper transformations that process and re-render content through different lenses — they may combine multiple strategies and affect the entire content presentation. See the dedicated section for:

• Perspective Pipeline — Positive/negative framing

• Emotional & Motivational Embedding — Virtue/vice-based framing

• Engagement Pipelines — Goal, pain point, interest associations

• Learning Style Transforms — Storytelling, analogies, technical styles
